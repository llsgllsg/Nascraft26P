Discord extension

Extend the market into your Discord server.

Note: this feature is in beta; it may be incomplete and may behave unexpectedly.

Nascraft ships a Discord extension that lets you create a bot opening the market to people on Discord.

Nascraft26P note: if your players are mostly on QQ, this fork also provides a QQ / OneBot v11 bridge (price quotes, holdings, trade announcements), see QQ bot. The Discord extension is still available and the two do not conflict.
