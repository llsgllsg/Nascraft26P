Price limits

Prices can be limited in two ways:

  • Hard limits: the price can never cross such a line. Configured per currency in config.yml.
  • Soft limits: the price may cross, but with noise enabled it is gradually pulled back into the range. The lower bound is called support, the upper bound resistance.

Hard limits (per currency):

currencies:
  # ...
  vault:
    # other options...
    # In this example every vault-priced item is bounded:
    # the price will never exceed 9999 and never drop below 0.05.
    top-limit: 9999
    low-limit: 0.05

  # other options...

Soft limits (per item):

items:
  cobblestone:

    # other properties...

    support: 0.01
    resistance: 0.1

Tuning tips

  • Hard limits are a safety valve; put them at values players can accept so item dupes cannot crash a price to zero.
  • Support / resistance shape the market range: keeping diamond support at a respectable level, for example, stops players from dumping it to the bottom.
  • Soft limits only act while noise is enabled; with noise off, only trades move the price.
