The Market

The market in Nascraft

The market is the core of the whole plugin, so understanding how it works matters.

In the real world everything has a value: wood, gold, even air. Those values are decided by the market — in other words, by supply, demand and how easy something is to get.

A few examples:

  • Wood: high demand, but supply is just as high, so it stays cheap.
  • Gold: high demand, low output, so it is expensive.
  • Air: everywhere, nearly worthless.

To imitate this in Minecraft the plugin uses a linear quote: each trade only moves the price a little, so many trades are needed before prices visibly move. That differs from the real world, where a price can jump at once without a lot of volume.

Why linear? A Minecraft server has no army of market makers, and running a full order book for every trade would be overkill; a linear quote also keeps price curves smooth instead of jumping around.

How players reach the market

Nascraft offers several channels:

  • Commands
    1. /sell
    2. /sellhand
    3. /sellall
  • The AdvancedGUI touch screen
  • The Discord extension
  • The /market command
  • Anything you build on our commands and placeholders
